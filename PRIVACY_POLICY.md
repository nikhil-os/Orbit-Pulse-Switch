## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy governs the use of the mobile game application **Orbit Pulse Switch** ("the Game") published by **KAMODI DEVI** ("Developer", "we", "us").

**Orbit Pulse Switch** is designed as a self-contained, 100% offline arcade reflex game. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal user information, device identifiers, or usage metrics.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Transmission
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, geographic locations, device identifiers, or financial information.
- **No Account Required:** Players can access and play the game immediately without any account creation, login, or registration.
- **No Internet Required / Zero Network Traffic:** The game runs purely offline and does not connect to external servers, APIs, or cloud infrastructure.
- **No Tracking:** We do not track user activities across applications or websites.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage (On-Device Only)
The application only saves minimal preference and score data strictly locally on your Android device (using local shared preferences):
1. **High Score** (Best score record)
2. **Total Runs** (Count of games played)
3. **Sound Settings** (Sound effects toggle state)
4. **Haptic Settings** (Vibration toggle state)

*None of this data is ever transmitted off the device.*

---

## 4. Third-Party SDKs and Services
To protect player privacy:
- **No Advertising SDKs** (No AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.)
- **No Analytics SDKs** (No Firebase, Google Analytics, Flurry, etc.)
- **No In-App Purchases (IAP)**
- **No Social Media Integration SDKs**

---

## 5. Device Permissions
Orbit Pulse Switch requests only the minimum required permission:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Enables optional haptic vibration feedback for collisions and button presses. Can be toggled on/off in the game settings.

The game does **not** access the camera, microphone, device storage, contacts, or location.

---

## 6. Google Play Data Safety Compliance
- **Data Collected:** None
- **Data Shared:** None
- **Data Security:** Offline operation guarantees no unencrypted or sensitive transmission.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy
Orbit Pulse Switch complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

---

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion
Since we do not store any personal data on external servers, there is no remote data retention. Users can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings (`Settings > Apps > Orbit Pulse Switch > Storage > Clear Storage`) or by uninstalling the app.
