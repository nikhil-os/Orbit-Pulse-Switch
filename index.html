Privacy Policy for Orbit Pulse Switch

Application: Orbit Pulse Switch (Package: com.tapblast.orbitpulseswitch)

Developer: KAMODI DEVI

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how KAMODI DEVI ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") manages user information in connection with the mobile application Orbit Pulse Switch ("the Game").

Core Privacy Commitment: Orbit Pulse Switch is a completely standalone offline arcade game. We do not collect, transmit, store, track, or share any personal information, sensitive user data, or device identifiers.

1. Information Collection and Transmission

Orbit Pulse Switch is built strictly with user privacy as a foundational principle:

  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or any government identifiers.
  • No Account Registration or Login: There is no account sign-in, authentication, or social login required.
  • No External Network Transmission: The game does not connect to any remote server, backend API, cloud database, or third-party web service. Gameplay runs entirely offline on your device.
  • No Location or Sensor Tracking: The application does not request, track, or access GPS or fine/coarse location data.

2. On-Device Local Data Storage

The game stores a minimal amount of configuration and gameplay progress locally on your Android device using standard local shared preferences:

  • High Score: Your best score achieved during gameplay.
  • Total Runs / Games Played: Local count of arcade attempts.
  • Audio Preferences: Toggle state for sound effects (enabled/disabled).
  • Haptic Preferences: Toggle state for vibration/haptics (enabled/disabled).

Note: All this data resides solely in local device storage assigned to the application. It is never uploaded to the internet or shared with any external server or service.

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To ensure absolute data protection and policy compliance:

  • No Advertising SDKs: The application does not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or any other commercial advertising network.
  • No Analytics SDKs: The application does not embed Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • No In-App Purchases (IAP): There are no digital billing libraries, payment processors, or financial transactions.
  • No Social Media SDKs: There are no social tracking or sharing integrations.

4. Device Permissions

Orbit Pulse Switch requests minimal, strictly necessary system permissions:

  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback during gameplay collisions and user actions. You can disable this anytime directly within the game's settings.

The game does not request access to the Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Photo Gallery, Storage/Media, Location, or Phone State.

5. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In adherence to Google Play's Data Safety declaration requirements:

  • Data Collection: No user data is collected.
  • Data Sharing: No user data is shared with third parties.
  • Security Practices: No data is transferred over an internet connection because no data is collected or transmitted.

6. Children’s Privacy

Orbit Pulse Switch is an arcade reflex game designed for general audiences. The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). It is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we never collect, receive, or store your personal data on external servers, there is no remote data to retain or delete.

You can clear all locally stored gameplay data (high scores and settings) at any time by clearing the application's storage in your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Orbit Pulse Switch > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with applicable laws or Google Play Developer Program Policies. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date".
